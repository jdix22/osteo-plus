Supporting the Perinatal Journey with Acupuncture, with Fanda Wong

By Justin Marco May 12, 2025 2 min read

At OSTEO+, we’re committed to thoughtful, holistic care for every stage of life, including the journey into parenthood. Fanda Wong, R.Ac., brings deep passion and experience to supporting women and birthing people through the perinatal period with gentle, personalized acupuncture.

Whether you’re planning a pregnancy, expecting, or navigating postpartum life, Fanda is here to support your health and well-being.

What is the perinatal period?

The perinatal period is the time before, during and after pregnancy, a phase that can bring both joy and challenge. Every person’s experience is unique, and supportive therapies can play a meaningful role.

Acupuncture during pregnancy

Your body goes through countless changes during pregnancy. Acupuncture, rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), is a gentle, non-invasive approach that may help support the body through this time. Clients often seek acupuncture for:

  • General wellness during pregnancy
  • Feelings of stress or anxiety
  • Occasional headaches or nausea
  • Difficulty falling or staying asleep
  • Overall energy levels

Each session is tailored to you, with an emphasis on comfort, calm and care.

Preparing for labour: the role of moxibustion

In the final weeks of pregnancy, moxibustion, a traditional technique that uses gentle heat from the herb mugwort, may be used to support the body’s natural rhythms as it prepares for labour.

Some people also seek this technique when their baby is in a breech position. Every journey is different, and many find moxibustion a relaxing, nurturing therapy late in pregnancy.

Postpartum recovery and support

The postpartum phase can be as demanding as it is beautiful. Fanda offers new parents a calm space for recovery and rebalancing. Postpartum acupuncture is often sought for:

  • General recovery after childbirth
  • Emotional well-being
  • Energy levels and fatigue
  • Muscle tension from feeding and carrying baby

Sessions are always conducted with sensitivity, compassion and respect for where you are in your journey.

Meet Fanda Wong, R.Ac.

Fanda is a Registered Acupuncturist in Ontario with a particular passion for women’s health and the perinatal experience. She has supported many people through fertility journeys, pregnancy and beyond. Her calm presence, evidence-informed practice and personalized care make her a trusted part of your wellness team.

Why choose OSTEO+?

Our integrated clinic brings together a compassionate team of practitioners so you feel welcomed, supported and seen. We serve clients across the Greater Toronto Area and welcome you to explore how acupuncture may fit into your perinatal care plan.
