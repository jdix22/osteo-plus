The Natural Botox Alternative: Lymphatic Drainage & Buccal Facial Massage

By Justin Marco June 2, 2026 4 min read

What are lymphatic drainage massage and buccal facial massage?

If you’ve been scrolling through wellness content lately, chances are you’ve come across two treatments taking the health and beauty world by storm: Brazilian lymphatic drainage massage and buccal facial massage. Once reserved for celebrity green rooms and high-end European spas, they are now recognized by health professionals and skincare enthusiasts alike as some of the most effective non-invasive therapies available.

At OSTEO+, our Registered Massage Therapist Andrea Pellegrino, RMT, specializes in both, bringing clinical expertise and a personalized approach to every treatment.

What is Brazilian lymphatic drainage?

Brazilian lymphatic drainage is a specialized massage technique that uses gentle, rhythmic strokes to stimulate the lymphatic system, the body’s natural detoxification network. Unlike deep tissue massage, it works superficially to encourage the movement of lymphatic fluid, helping the body flush out toxins, reduce inflammation and eliminate excess fluid retention.

Originally developed in Brazil and popularized through social media and celebrity endorsements, it has become one of the most sought-after wellness treatments of the decade.

Benefits of lymphatic drainage massage

  • Reduces puffiness and bloating by eliminating fluid build-up beneath the skin
  • Supports post-surgical recovery: widely used after procedures like liposuction, tummy tucks and BBL surgery to help healing and reduce swelling
  • Boosts immune function: a healthy lymphatic system is foundational to immune response
  • Improves skin tone and texture through better circulation
  • Relieves fatigue and promotes relaxation: the nervous system response to lymphatic work is deeply calming
  • Reduces the appearance of cellulite as fluid moves better over time

Whether you’re recovering from a procedure, dealing with chronic puffiness, or simply want to feel lighter and more energized, lymphatic drainage massage at OSTEO+ delivers visible results.

What is buccal facial massage?

Buccal facial massage (also called intraoral massage) is an advanced facial technique where the therapist works on both the outside and inside of the mouth to reach the deep muscles of the face, jaw and cheeks, muscles that no traditional facial, roller or gua sha tool can effectively reach.

“Buccal” comes from the Latin word for cheek. The treatment targets the buccinator muscle and surrounding tissue, releasing deeply held tension while sculpting and lifting the face from within.

Why everyone is talking about buccal facial massage

Buccal facial massage has been dubbed “the natural Botox,” and for good reason. Botox works by freezing facial muscles to reduce movement and lines; buccal massage works the opposite way, releasing chronically contracted muscles and restoring their natural tone and position. The result is a lifted, more sculpted, naturally youthful look without a single needle.

Benefits of buccal facial massage

  • Natural lifting and contouring: releases tight muscles that pull the face downward
  • Softens fine lines and wrinkles, especially around the mouth, jaw and cheeks
  • Relieves jaw tension and TMJ symptoms, great for people who clench or grind
  • Improves lymphatic drainage in the face: less puffiness, under-eye bags and facial bloating
  • Stimulates collagen production through increased circulation
  • Headache and tension relief for many clients with chronic tension headaches
  • A needle-free alternative to Botox and fillers, with results that look natural because they are

Combining lymphatic drainage and buccal massage

Together, the two treatments work on body and face at once. The lymphatic work reduces inflammation and improves circulation from head to toe, while the buccal facial work refines and sculpts the face with precision.

Andrea’s training as a Registered Massage Therapist means she brings a deep understanding of anatomy, fascia and soft tissue to every treatment, going well beyond a typical spa facial. Each session is tailored to your anatomy, concerns and goals.

  • Registered, clinically trained expertise, not just a spa service
  • Treatment tailored to your body and skincare goals
  • A safe, professional setting inside a multidisciplinary health clinic
  • Lasting results grounded in anatomy and therapeutic technique

Who is a good candidate?

Lymphatic drainage and buccal facial massage suit people who:

  • Experience chronic facial puffiness or bloating
  • Want natural anti-aging options without injectables
  • Have jaw tension, teeth grinding or TMJ dysfunction
  • Are recovering from surgery and want less swelling
  • Want to improve skin tone, texture and overall facial appearance
  • Prefer a holistic approach to beauty and wellness
