Prenatal Massage: Benefits, Safety and Costs for Expecting Mothers

By Justin Marco January 16, 2025 3 min read

Pregnancy can be an exciting journey, but it comes with its own challenges, like body aches, joint swelling and fatigue. For expecting mothers, prenatal massage is a natural way to find relief, ease sore muscles and relax before your little one arrives.

What is prenatal massage?

Prenatal massage is a therapeutic technique tailored to the needs of pregnant people. Unlike a regular massage, it focuses on easing discomfort associated with pregnancy. According to Anna Guimadeev, a Registered Massage Therapist in Thornhill, prenatal massage is designed to address the physical changes and complications that arise as pregnancy progresses.

A skilled therapist customizes the treatment to your trimester and any underlying issues, keeping both mother and baby comfortable and safe.

Benefits of prenatal massage

  • Relieves back pain. As your centre of gravity shifts, massage targets the lower back, which takes extra strain.
  • Decreases joint pain and swelling. Gentle massage and lymphatic drainage techniques can reduce swelling in the hips, knees and ankles.
  • Reduces nerve pain. Loosening tight muscles can ease pressure on nerves.
  • Relieves tension headaches by promoting relaxation and reducing muscle tension.
  • Improves circulation, delivering more oxygen and nutrients for baby’s growth.
  • Balances stress hormones, lowering cortisol and norepinephrine and boosting serotonin and dopamine.
  • Promotes better sleep by easing physical discomfort.
  • Reduces stress and anxiety with its calming effect.

Is prenatal massage safe during pregnancy?

Prenatal massage is safe for most women, but it’s wise to take a cautious approach. Consult your doctor or obstetrician before booking, and make sure your therapist is experienced in prenatal techniques.

Some therapists avoid massage in the first trimester, and deep tissue work on the legs is often avoided because of the risk of blood clots. As your belly grows, a trained therapist uses specially designed pillows and bolsters to keep you positioned safely and comfortably.

When to skip a prenatal massage

Avoid massage if you experience:

  • Bleeding
  • Pre-term contractions
  • A high-risk pregnancy
  • Pregnancy-induced hypertension
  • Preeclampsia
  • Previous pre-term labour
  • Severe swelling or high blood pressure
  • Sudden, severe headaches

Always consult your doctor before booking and tell your therapist about any underlying conditions.

How much does prenatal massage cost?

A prenatal massage generally costs between $60 and $200, depending on the therapist’s experience and the length of the session. Check whether your health insurance covers it; some plans reimburse part or all of the cost.

Many women find prenatal massage most helpful later in pregnancy, when discomfort intensifies. Spread out your sessions and ask your therapist how often to come in.

Questions to ask your prenatal massage therapist

  • Are you certified and registered to provide prenatal massage?
  • Do you have experience working with pregnant clients?
  • What oils and essential oils do you use? (Some aren’t recommended early in pregnancy.)
  • Are there techniques I should know about for my pregnancy?

Find a prenatal massage therapist near you

When choosing a therapist, look at reviews, qualifications and cost. OSTEO+ offers pre-natal massage in Thornhill.

Final thoughts

Prenatal massage is a safe and effective way to relieve pregnancy-related discomfort, reduce stress and improve overall well-being. Whether you’re dealing with back pain, swelling or anxiety, it may offer the relief you need to enjoy a more comfortable pregnancy.
