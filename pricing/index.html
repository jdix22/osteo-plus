Pricing

Treatment prices at OSTEO+

Sessions start at $80 for 30 minutes of acupuncture or fascial stretch therapy. Osteopathy is $100–$155, massage therapy $90–$175, and physiotherapy $100–$150. Prices match our online booking page. Acupuncture, naturopathy and physiotherapy have no HST; HST applies to other services based on the service.

Free 15-minute consultations

An initial consultation at no charge is available for osteopathy, acupuncture and athletic therapy. Our naturopathic doctor offers a free 15-minute phone consultation.

Insurance

Coverage depends on your plan, but most insurance companies cover osteopathic treatment. RMT massage comes with RMT receipts. Check your plan for details.

Cupping & taping

Cupping and kinesiology taping are used within other treatments, such as osteopathy, massage, acupuncture and athletic therapy, rather than booked on their own.
