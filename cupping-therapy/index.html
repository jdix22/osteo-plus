What is cupping therapy?

Cupping is a type of alternative therapy that involves placing cups on the skin to create suction. Cupping increases blood circulation to the area where the cups are placed.

This may relieve muscle tension, which can improve overall blood flow and promote cell repair. It may also help form new connective tissue and create new blood vessels in the tissue.

At OSTEO+, cupping is used as part of osteopathy, registered massage therapy, therapeutic sports massage and acupuncture sessions. Book one of those treatments and mention you would like cupping.

What to expect after treatment

The circular marks usually fade within 5–10 days. Mild itchiness over the marks is normal.

Keep the treated area covered, dry and warm, and avoid wind, cold and damp.

Included in treatment

Cupping is added within osteopathy, massage, therapeutic sports massage or acupuncture sessions.
