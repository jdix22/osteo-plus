About OSTEO+

A Thornhill clinic built around chronic pain and wellness

OSTEO+ is an osteopathy clinic at 10 Disera Dr, Unit 200 in Thornhill, Ontario, co-founded by osteopath and RMT Anna Guimadeev, with osteopath Justin Marco as CEO and clinic director.

Our commitment

At OSTEO+, we are committed to providing compassionate and effective therapy to help our clients overcome life’s challenges and reach their goals. Our team of highly skilled and motivated practitioners helps individuals, couples and families through difficult times toward hope, healing and growth.

We are dedicated to personalized, effective osteopathic care that helps clients achieve optimal health and wellness. Together, we deliver the highest standard of care to every client who joins the OSTEO+ family, helping them unlock their body’s full potential.

  • Whole-body

    Osteopathy looks for the root cause, not just the sore spot, and the rest of the team works the same way.

  • Under one roof

    Osteopathy, physio, athletic therapy, massage, acupuncture and naturopathy, so your care isn’t in a silo.

  • Personal

    Every plan is built around your history, your goals and how your body responds.
