Frequently asked questions

About osteopathy

What is osteopathy?

Osteopathy is a manual therapy that aims to improve health by restoring the body’s structural balance and increasing its ability to heal itself. It uses gentle manual techniques to assess and treat a wide range of conditions, including musculoskeletal pain, joint problems and headaches.

Who can benefit from osteopathic treatment?

Osteopathic treatment can help people of all ages and backgrounds. It is suitable for acute and chronic conditions, and for people looking to improve their overall health and wellness.

What conditions can osteopathy treat?

Osteopathy can treat a wide range of conditions, including back and neck pain, joint problems, headaches, sports injuries and postural imbalances. It can also help with digestive and respiratory issues, and with problems related to pregnancy and childbirth.

What should I expect during an osteopathic treatment?

At your first appointment, your osteopath carries out a thorough examination and asks about your symptoms, lifestyle and medical history. From that they build a personalized treatment plan, which may include manual techniques such as stretching, massage and joint mobilization.

Is osteopathic treatment safe?

Yes, osteopathic treatment is safe when performed by a qualified, registered practitioner. Your osteopath takes your individual needs and health into account and adjusts the intensity of treatment accordingly.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your circumstances and how severe your condition is. Your osteopath can give you a more accurate estimate after your first appointment.

Can I have osteopathic treatment with a pre-existing medical condition?

Yes. Tell your osteopath about any medical conditions you have, as they may need to adjust their approach.

Is osteopathic treatment covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by provider, so check your plan for details, although most insurance companies cover osteopathic treatment.

Visiting OSTEO+

Where is OSTEO+ and when are you open?

OSTEO+ is at 10 Disera Dr, Unit 200, Thornhill, ON L4J 0A7, on the second floor. We are open Monday to Thursday 10 am – 8 pm, Friday 10 am – 2 pm and Saturday 9 am – 2 pm.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online any time through our Jane booking page, where you can choose a practitioner, treatment and time. You can also call (289) 588-3124 or email info@osteo-plus.ca.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. A free 15-minute initial consultation is available for osteopathy, acupuncture and athletic therapy, and our Naturopathic Doctor offers a free 15-minute phone consultation. Choose “Initial Consultation” when you book online.

How should I prepare for my first visit?

Fill out the online intake form and create your profile before you arrive, bring or upload any X-ray, ultrasound or MRI reports, wear loose comfortable clothing, avoid a big meal 2–3 hours before, and arrive on time so you get your full session.

Do RMTs provide insurance receipts?

Yes. Massage therapy with our Registered Massage Therapists, including lymphatic drainage and buccal facial massage, is provided with RMT receipts you can submit to your insurance.
