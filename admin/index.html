    How publishing works

    Edits stay on this page until you press Publish to website. Publishing saves everything in one go, and the live site updates within a minute or two. Closing this page before publishing loses unpublished edits.

    Writing tips that help people find the post

    • Put the treatment and “Thornhill” in the title when it reads naturally, e.g. “Acupuncture for Migraines in Thornhill”.
    • Start with a one-sentence answer, then the detail. Use subheadings that are the questions patients ask.
    • Share what only your team knows: how you treat it, how many sessions it usually takes, what a visit is like.
    • Pick the practitioner who wrote or checked it, so readers can book with them.
