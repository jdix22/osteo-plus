Your first visit

What happens when you book

  1. 1

    Book online

    Choose a practitioner and time on our Jane booking page, or call the front desk at (289) 588-3124.

  2. 2

    Fill in your intake

    Complete the online intake form and profile before you arrive. Upload any X-ray, ultrasound or MRI reports.

  3. 3

    Come comfortable

    Wear loose clothing you can move in, skip a big meal 2–3 hours before, and arrive on time for your full session.

  4. 4

    Assessment & treatment

    Your practitioner reviews your history, examines you and builds a plan around the root cause.

  5. 5

    Aftercare

    Hydrate, sleep well and keep movement gentle. Some soreness for a day or two is normal.

All FAQs

What is osteopathy?

Osteopathy is a manual therapy that aims to improve health by restoring the body’s structural balance and increasing its ability to heal itself. It uses gentle manual techniques to assess and treat a wide range of conditions, including musculoskeletal pain, joint problems and headaches.

What conditions can osteopathy treat?

Osteopathy can treat a wide range of conditions, including back and neck pain, joint problems, headaches, sports injuries and postural imbalances. It can also help with digestive and respiratory issues, and with problems related to pregnancy and childbirth.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. A free 15-minute initial consultation is available for osteopathy, acupuncture and athletic therapy, and our Naturopathic Doctor offers a free 15-minute phone consultation. Choose “Initial Consultation” when you book online.

Is osteopathic treatment covered by insurance?

Coverage varies by provider, so check your plan for details, although most insurance companies cover osteopathic treatment.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your circumstances and how severe your condition is. Your osteopath can give you a more accurate estimate after your first appointment.
